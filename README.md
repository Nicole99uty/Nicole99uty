## About me

I'm a third-year PhD student in Quantitative Marketing at NUS. My background combines computer science, statistics, and machine learning with research on consumers and marketing.

I work on multimodal AI for understanding advertising creatives and predicting their effectiveness, using audio, visual, text, and human-feedback data. I'm interested in connecting computational methods with meaningful questions about how people respond to marketing.

## What I bring

- **A foundation in both computing and statistics.** My undergraduate training at UBC and master's training at NUS help me connect implementation, modelling, and statistical reasoning.
- **AI grounded in marketing questions.** I approach advertising research through both the content of a creative and the consumer responses it generates.
- **Careful evaluation.** My work includes supervised and representation learning, nested and out-of-fold cross-validation, randomized consumer experiments, and A/B testing.
- **Experience with real business problems.** My internships at Aibee and Zuoyebang involved advertising performance, conversion funnels, user segmentation, and commercialization strategy.
